Zero Waste Goal
The Problem

Bakeries guess. Retailers wait. Bread gets wasted.

Every night, bakeries bake based on instinct. Every morning, retailers hope their bread arrives. Both sides lose money.

The Old Way

Manual orders, phone calls, and guesswork lead to waste and frustration.

  • Bakeries bake too much → unsold bread thrown away
  • Bakeries bake too little → retailers run out
  • Orders taken by phone → errors and no records
  • No data on what sells → can't plan production
  • Cash and credit tracked in notebooks → lost money

The LoafLink Way

A simple digital system that tells the bakery exactly what to bake — every single night.

  • Retailers order on their phone before 8:00 PM
  • System locks orders → bakery gets an exact Bake List
  • Auto-generated production sheet at 8:05 PM
  • WhatsApp/SMS confirms every order instantly
  • Full history, reports, and zero guesswork
